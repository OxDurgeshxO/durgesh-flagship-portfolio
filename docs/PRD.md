# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Target Audience
- **Recruiters & Engineering Managers**: Rapid validation of skills, credentials, and track record.
- **AI/ML Engineers & Architects**: Interactive proof of client-side ML, computer vision, and WebGL architecture.

## Core Capabilities
- **Flagship Case Studies**: Deep technical breakdowns of RoleRadar, FitTrack, and MarketMatch-AI.
- **CyberBot AI Companion**: Procedural 3D assistant with speech synthesis, voice recognition, semantic Q&A, and chassis skins.
- **Interactive AI Lab**: Real-time client-side ML execution (pose estimation, clustering).
- **Recruiter Matrix & Resume**: Instant PDF download, verified credentials, and skills taxonomy.
- **Enterprise Edge Deployment**: Cloudflare Pages with strict security headers, zero-trust sanitization, and sub-second TTFB.
