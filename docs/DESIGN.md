# Design System Specification

## Aesthetic Foundation
- **Style**: Cyber-Dark Minimalist with High-Luminescence Accents.
- **Backgrounds**: Deep Obsidian (`#030712`, `#07050e`, `#09090b`).
- **Surfaces**: Frosted glassmorphism (`backdrop-blur-md bg-slate-950/80 border border-cyan-500/20`).

## CyberBot Chassis Skin System
- **Cobalt Prime**: Cyan `#00f0ff`, Eye Glow `#38bdf8`, Reactor `#0284c7`.
- **Emerald Core**: Neon Mint `#10b981`, Eye Glow `#34d399`, Reactor `#059669`.
- **Amethyst Void**: Neon Purple `#a855f7`, Eye Glow `#c084fc`, Reactor `#7c3aed`.
- **Solar Flare**: Amber Gold `#f59e0b`, Eye Glow `#fbbf24`, Reactor `#d97706`.

## Typography & Focus States
- Font: Inter / Geist Sans paired with JetBrains Mono for telemetry codes.
- Focus Rings: `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-cyan-400 focus-visible:outline-none`.
